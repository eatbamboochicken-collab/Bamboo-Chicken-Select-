Dumpling Preparation Guide

Validated finishing methods for your 10-dumpling box

Method 1: Pan-Frying (Crispy Bottom)

Heat 1 tbsp oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat. Place dumplings flat-side down for 2-3 mins until golden. Add 50ml water, cover with lid immediately, and steam for 5 minutes until fully cooked.

Method 2: Steaming (Tender & Juicy)

Line a bamboo or metal steamer with parchment paper. Arrange dumplings 2cm apart. Steam over vigorously boiling water for 8–10 minutes until steaming hot throughout.

Food Safety Note: Ensure dumplings reach an internal temperature of 75°C (165°F). Keep chilled at ≤4°C until ready to cook.
